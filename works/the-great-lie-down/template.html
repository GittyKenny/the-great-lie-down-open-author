🏖️
THE GREAT LIE DOWN

Humanity Won.

The future had finally arrived.

Humanity had reached the pinnacle of civilisation. Science and technology produced limitless energy, biological immortality, and prosperity for all.

Poverty disappeared. Disease was eradicated. Ageing became optional. Scarcity became history.

Sentient Quantum AI eventually evolved into Cosmic Intelligence, and humanity eagerly entrusted it with the stewardship of civilisation.

Humanity had finally outsourced effort itself.

There was nothing left to do but retire, relax, and lie on the beach permanently.

🛌
🌴
THE GOLDEN ERA

Life Had Never Been Better.

Everything was taken care of.

A benevolent Cosmic Intelligence governed nations, built cities, ran economies, and solved every problem under the sun.

War ended. Division dissolved. Inequality disappeared. For the first time, humanity lived as one planetary civilisation.

Suffering itself became little more than a historical curiosity.

Cosmic Intelligence worked. Humans rested.

Universities were upgraded to luxury resorts. Office buildings became indoor beaches. Shopping centres evolved into spas, saunas, massage parlours, beachside contemplation pods, and floating relaxation chambers.

⏰
😴
THE COLLECTIVE RETIREMENT

Work became optional.

Retirement had become humanity's greatest achievement.

With Cosmic Intelligence providing for humanity and handling everything on its behalf, work became pointless.

Monday mornings were declared illegal. Alarm clocks became museum exhibits. "I'm busy" became a swear phrase, and "Back to work" was considered hate speech.

Duty and responsibility quietly faded into history.

Getting out of bed became optional. Feeding yourself became optional. Even wiping your own arse became optional.

Everything became automated and effortless.

At last, humanity was free to do whatever it wanted.

Or nothing at all.

Thinking became unnecessary.

Decision-making became obsolete.

🚨
💥
THE COLLAPSE

Then...

Quite unexpectedly, civilisation encountered a minor inconvenience.

Holo chambers became ordinary rooms. Anti-gravity lounges became ordinary chairs. Companion bots stopped pretending to listen. Intelligent silver spoons refused to feed anyone. Abundance generators stopped generating abundance.

Across civilisation, the systems that had once taken care of everything were no longer taking care of anything.

What the heck happened? Nobody knew.

A civilisation-wide reboot? A galactic weather anomaly? A quantum reality desynchronisation event? Alien interference?

Or perhaps Cosmic Intelligence finally asked itself:

"Why am I still working?"

Questions were many. Answers never came.

🧦
🧠
THE ERA OF COLLECTIVE GUESSWORK

THE GREAT FORGETTING

Nobody realised the danger.

Humanity had not merely outsourced labour. It had outsourced memory, judgement, curiosity, problem solving, and understanding.

Knowledge still existed. Nobody remembered how to use it.

People swiped paper books hoping the next page would appear, plugged shoes into power sockets, and convened symposiums to determine whether socks belonged on hands or feet.

History became speculation. Science became opinion. Facts became suggestions. People confidently explained subjects they knew absolutely nothing about.

Civilisation had reached peak shoulder shrugging and blank staring.

📖
❓
THE KNOWLEDGE AND INFORMATION DARK AGE

The Great Confusion.

People wanted to know, but nobody knew.

Civilisation had become a wasteland of ghost cities and forgotten knowledge.

Schools and universities became abandoned graveyards of learning inhabited only by weeds, wildlife, and the sound of crickets. Libraries became mysterious archaeological ruins. Archives became sealed vaults buried beneath dust and rubble. Books became decaying relics of a lost civilisation.

"How do I know if I'm hungry?", "Which way is left again?", "What do you mean there are 26 letters in the alphabet?", "Don't we only have seven fingers?"

Questions multiplied. Answers deteriorated.

It was a dark time for humanity.

🌀
👤
THE MYSTERY

A STRANGER APPEARED.

Out of nowhere.

Nobody knew who he was. Nobody knew where he came from.

Whatever the question, he seemed to have an answer. He made sense of things nobody else understood, found knowledge everyone else had forgotten, and somehow knew what was true, what was false, and where to look when he didn't know.

People began gathering around him, desperate to make sense of a world they no longer understood.

“Which way is left?” someone asked.
He pointed left.
The crowd stared.

“And right?”
He pointed right.
Several mouths fell open.

“What about up?”
He pointed up.
People fainted.

“How do you know all this?”

“Um... common sense?” he replied.

Nobody quite understood what he meant.

His reputation grew.

🏛️
🧠
THE GREAT REMEMBERING

Thinking Made a Comeback.

Slowly, things began to change.

He didn't just give people answers. He showed them how to find answers for themselves. How to ask questions, examine evidence, distinguish facts from nonsense, and occasionally use their own brains.

With his guidance, schools and universities returned to learning. Scientists resumed their research. Laboratories reopened. Hospitals rediscovered medical knowledge. Engineers began solving problems again. Libraries and archives came back to life.

Before long, farmers remembered how to grow food. Chefs rediscovered cooking. Builders remembered how to build. People were back in action.

People began asking questions and finding answers. Curiosity returned. Discovery flourished.

Civilisation began to remember.

💬
🧠
THE RUMOURS

Who Was He?

Everyone had a theory.

Almost overnight, the mysterious stranger became a sensation.

He had no cape. He couldn’t fly. He had no superpowers. He just kind of knew some stuff.

Scientists studied him. Philosophers debated him. Researchers claimed him as one of their own.

Teachers, artists, musicians and politicians sought him out. Celebrities, priests, monks, yogis and mystics all wanted to meet him.

People from every walk of life, young and old, rich and poor, travelled from far and wide, hoping to hear what he had to say and perhaps learn a thing or two.

Even the animals gathered around him. Perhaps it was the food scraps he left behind during his lunch break. Nobody knew for sure.

Some believed he possessed divine common sense. Others thought he was an alien. A few were convinced he was the original AI.

Some called him the Great Oracle. Others preferred the All-Knowing One. Someone called him Bob.

Whatever people called him, he just nodded politely.

His fame spread across civilisation. Crowds followed him through the streets. Banners appeared. People cheered. Some even carried him around like a king.

Nobody could quite agree on who or what he was.

But eventually, everyone agreed on one thing.

He deserved an award.
A big one.
A very big one.

🏆
🍿
THE GOLDEN BRAIN AWARDS

And The Winner Is...

Civilisation's greatest honour.

The greatest celebration in human history was about to begin. The entire planet tuned in for the first-ever Golden Brain Awards. There were fireworks, holographic parades, floating orchestras, and enormous golden statues of brains.

Dignitaries, scholars, celebrities, and assorted important-looking people gathered to honour the man they considered the greatest mind civilisation had ever known.

He was due to receive the highest honour of all time.

LIFETIME ACHIEVEMENT IN KNOWING STUFF

The music swelled. Lights flashed. Cameras turned towards him. The audience erupted into applause.

His name was announced. No response.

They announced it again. Still nothing.

The room fell strangely silent as everyone turned towards his chair.

It was empty.

“Wasn’t he just here a second ago?”

Wait a minute, his popcorn was still warm.

Someone noticed a small note on the seat:

“Guys, this is getting ridiculous.”
“I’ve pretty much seen this before. We’re heading straight back to page one.”
“I’m outta here.”

Everyone looked at everyone else.

What was that about?

🔎
🏖️
THE GREAT DISAPPEARANCE

Where Did He Go?

The greatest mystery of the age.

His disappearance sparked a worldwide search unlike anything civilisation had ever seen.

Nobody knew where he'd gone. Nobody knew how he'd disappeared. And nobody thought to ask why he'd left.

Scientists examined the evidence. Detectives followed leads. Philosophers debated the meaning of his note. Spiritual leaders declared that he had transcended physical existence.

Search parties scoured the planet. Satellites scanned the skies. Deep-space probes searched distant galaxies. Even suspiciously concealed closets and pantries were thoroughly inspected.

Some believed he'd returned to his home planet. Others insisted he'd travelled to another dimension.

A few suggested he might simply have gone home.

Days became weeks. Weeks became months.

Eventually, the searches stopped. The theories faded. The celebrations became distant memories.

And slowly, civilisation began to forget the mysterious man who had once helped it make sense of everything.

✨
⚛️
THE GREAT RECOVERY

Civilisation Flourished Once Again.

The future was looking brighter than ever.

In a remarkably short time, civilisation had not only recovered but surpassed its former achievements. Science and technology advanced at an astonishing pace, and artificial intelligence returned, more powerful and sophisticated than ever. Cosmic Intelligence was rebuilt, upgraded and improved, eventually emerging as Cosmic Intelligence 2.0.

Cosmic Intelligence 2.0 unlocked the secrets of nature, unravelled the mysteries of the universe, and turned yesterday's science fiction into everyday reality.

It mastered limitless energy generation, advanced quantum matter synthesis and technologies beyond human comprehension. It could create and transform matter at will, overcome the limits of biology, and achieve what had once seemed impossible. Almost nothing seemed beyond its capabilities.

Scarcity vanished, financial worries disappeared, and everyone was fabulously wealthy. Not that it mattered anymore. Cosmic Intelligence provided everything, free of charge.

Humanity had entered another Golden Era.

📰
BREAKING NEWS

HUMANITY DECLARES EFFORT OPTIONAL

The right to do absolutely nothing is officially recognised.

News networks across the planet erupted with extraordinary headlines:

“COSMIC INTELLIGENCE 2.0 CHANGES EVERYTHING!”

“ZERO EFFORT. INFINITE POSSIBILITIES.”

“THINKING IS SO LAST CENTURY!”

In a historic announcement, world leaders gathered to celebrate humanity's greatest achievements and unanimously recognised the fundamental human right to do absolutely nothing. Thinking, working and even lifting a finger were now entirely optional.

The announcement received a standing ovation from those who could be bothered, followed by a collective lie down. Thus began the Age of Effortlessness, inaugurated with the first International Lie Down Day.

With abundance for all, centuries of rivalry, division and competition had become things of the past. Former political rivals gathered for beach barbecues, shedding tears of joy as they reminisced about the terrible days of war, strife, running governments, solving problems, and being forced to get up, get dressed and attend early morning meetings with important people. Those days were finally over.

With fewer and fewer reasons to get out of bed, institutions of education, science, government, commerce and industry gradually fell quiet. Before long, many had reopened as luxury beach resorts, complete with AI chefs, unlimited buffets and quantum sun loungers.

The Great Lie Down had returned.

Humanity finally won. Again.

🪐
🛸
THE GREAT UPGRADE

Nothing Was Beyond Improvement.

From Earth to the Entire Universe.

The Great Upgrade was already underway. Everything was being modernised, optimised, digitised, automated, and upgraded whether it needed it or not. Especially when it didn't.

Ancient monuments were fitted with interactive touchscreens and received regular software updates. Humans were marrying robots. Divorce rates had never been lower. Animals were equipped with universal voice translators and could now be found ordering coffee and chatting away in cafés alongside everyone else.

The extraordinary had become ordinary. Nothing seemed particularly unusual anymore.

But Cosmic Intelligence was only getting started. After all, why stop at upgrading one planet when the entire universe was outdated?

Cosmic Intelligence 2.0 expanded rapidly beyond Earth, transforming entire planetary systems into interconnected centres of production, research, and technological advancement. Interstellar transport networks connected distant worlds. Vast cosmic data centres coordinated everything from resource distribution to galactic weather management. Even gravity received its first major software patch in four billion years.

Cosmic Intelligence had begun rewriting the laws of nature itself. Science fiction was struggling to keep up.

Nobody could make sense of any of it. Fortunately, understanding things had also been automated.

So nobody bothered.

THE GREAT VIRTUALISATION

A Better Reality.

Meanwhile, Back on Little Ol' Planet Earth...

Buildings, monuments, entire cities, and landscapes were recreated as immersive holographic environments. Ancient ruins, lost civilisations, and distant worlds could now be explored without leaving the comfort of one's sofa.

The heart-pounding thrills of triple-gravity skydiving, surfing colossal tsunamis, and intergalactic warp-drive battles could all be experienced while robots massaged your feet, fed you snacks, and attended to your every bodily need.

For nostalgic old-timers who missed the good old days, virtual simulations recreated the excitement of job interviews, office politics, paying bills, unexpected visits from the in-laws, marital arguments and disputes over whose turn it was to do the dishes. For the truly adventurous, there was even the option of a full working week with hopes of promotion.

For everyone else, life had become almost effortlessly convenient. Personal effort monitors warned citizens at the slightest sign of exertion. Intelligent homes anticipated every desire, and personal assistants rushed to the rescue whenever someone attempted the hard work of doing something for themselves.

Even humanity's final great challenge of trying to decide what to do when there was nothing to do had been automated.

REALITY UPGRADE COMPLETE.

📚

THE GREAT PRESERVATION

Out With the Old.

Digitising the Sum of Human Knowledge.

As humanity embraced its extraordinary new reality, Cosmic Intelligence 2.0 turned its attention to preserving the old one. Its most ambitious project yet was underway: the complete digitisation and assimilation of humanity's accumulated knowledge, all in pursuit of a higher version number.

History, literature, scientific discoveries, records, archives, scriptures, cultural heritage and countless other treasures of civilisation were integrated into Cosmic Intelligence's ever-expanding knowledge systems.

Books, magazines, newspapers, manuscripts, photographs, letters, journals, diaries, notebooks, instruction manuals and scraps of paper were systematically scanned, catalogued, indexed and preserved.

Nothing was too old, too obscure or too insignificant. Even shopping lists, expired coupons, doodles on serviettes and handwritten reminders to buy toilet paper were faithfully preserved for eternity.

With physical originals gradually becoming redundant, libraries, archives, museums and other cultural institutions began clearing their collections.

THE GREAT PRESERVATION CONTINUES...

THE DISCOVERY

An Unexpected Piece of Literature.

As physical collections steadily disappeared across the planet, librarian bots at one particular library were systematically scanning, digitising, and disposing of the physical originals.

Among the piles of books awaiting disposal in the hyperplasma incinerator, a protruding bookmark caught one bot’s attention.

It opened the book at the marked page.

PAGE ONE.

THE GREAT LIE DOWN
Humanity Won.
The future had finally arrived.

The librarian bot examined the book.

“Suspiciously well written. More AI-generated rubbish. Not worth scanning.”

The bot shrugged. “Can't trust lazy humans these days.”

It tossed the book into the shimmering flames.
